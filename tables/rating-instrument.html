Table F.1. Program year 2026 rating form for the Small City, Large City, and County funds

ComponentPointsEvidence assessed
Needs assessment5Community-wide need and low- and moderate-income conditions.
Project development45Need, alternatives, activities, and costs; includes 10 points for project maps.
Impact50Actual beneficiaries, directness, and urgency.
Other considerations30Operations, maintenance, participation, implementation capacity, and distress.
Nature of benefits130Subtotal of the four components above.
Cost-benefit ratio50Requested dollars per actual beneficiary.
Matching contribution20Local match; full score for applicants with populations of 1,000 or fewer, or projects with 100% LMI beneficiaries.
Overall cap200Total score, subject to the form's provisions.

Note. The 45-point Project Development category includes 10 points for maps. Nature of Benefits is the 130-point subtotal, not an additional category. The overall cap is 200. Bonus and adjustment provisions are outside this summary; completed project scores are unobserved. LMI means low and moderate income.
